## Review resolutions

1. **Historical bytes:** distinguish absent contract from explicit empty contract. Serialize absent without a key; do not rewrite stored canonical payloads, inject defaults or relabel prior validations. Older clients reject the added closed field; capability advertisement/client upgrade must precede new payload production. Old binaries cannot safely consume new contract-bearing universes. No dual unrestricted downgrade route.
2. **Migration:** touched records need contracts; adding one revisits all bound events. A new action identity is the simplest way to preserve different historical intended mechanics. Retaining untouched legacy events is an explicit scope exclusion, not broad G03/G04 completion. No hidden migration job or retrospective repair.
3. **Authority:** definition permission and protected author approval are distinct gates in current code. Ordinary delegate may perform contracted events only inside all effect/interval-update boundaries with fresh complete validation. Proposal author review must expose the contract change as a retcon.
4. **Invalidation:** full candidate revalidation already exists, but consequence dependencies/evaluation are new. Snapshot/proposal digest and head checks protect stale publication; evidence versions and diagnostic accounting must bind the execution result. Runtime integration tests must prove no serialization omission drops those details.
5. **Independent proof limit:** two arithmetic algorithms do not prove service integration, namespace scanner behavior, historical serializer changes, visibility or native review. All are frozen future fixtures. The proposal adds no runtime files and no design approval.

No flaw in the accepted owner-attribute design was established; D02 specifies its documented missing consequence feature. Scope equality, direct resource evidence and interacting-key checks are proposed extensions, not claims about existing enforcement.
