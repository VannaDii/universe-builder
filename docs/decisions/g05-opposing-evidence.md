# G05 decision: opposing evidence

October 2, 2026. **Proposed; pending Vanna's decision.** This is the concrete D01/G05 proposal, not an approved ADR, implementation, or G05 closure claim. Scope: automatic detection of support/refutation overlap and its authored admission policy. No manuscript interpretation, knowledge acquisition, global cardinality, action effects, or new approval authority.

## Decision requested

Approve or revise this recommendation: always report definite opposing evidence in the submitted candidate, including equivalent propositions with different record IDs. Retain both polarities. Reporting alone is nonblocking; an explicitly authored, versioned consistency rule may block in its declared context/world/time. Preserve exact author approval, protected-definition controls, delegation limits and version-bound exceptions. Unresolved comparison is visible coverage, not a clean result or a contradiction proved by guesswork.

The tradeoff is additional findings and occasional partial coverage, plus a small rule-language and result-contract extension. Existing objective inconsistency does not become universally illegal. An author wishing to prohibit it must author the blocking policy. This recommendation needs Vanna's decision before affected implementation.

## Verified baseline and both mechanisms

Current source, rather than historical completion claims, establishes:

- `validation.rs::scalar_conflicts` compares only Support assertions and checks different values for single/exclusive properties. It cannot catch positive and negative evidence about the *same* value. Preserve that separate existing check, including its admission behavior.
- `basic.rs::claim` accumulates two independent evidence bits, including assertions attached to different matching proposition IDs, when an executable rule asks for the claim. There is no automatic candidate-wide opposing-evidence report when no rule consumes it. Its query matching is not a safe equivalence key: it can omit value/roles and does not compare quantification.
- `workflow.rs::evaluate` independently runs scalar/completeness checks, makes their conflicts and unresolved gaps inadmissible, and emits rule findings. Do not attach G05 reporting to that unconditional scalar denial branch.
- `rule_checks.rs` uses exact finite temporal frames and scoped exceptions; original evidence remains after waiver. `workflow.rs::approve` rejects an inadmissible checked candidate even for the author. Commit independently checks digest, evaluator, authority, head and fresh private validation. The word “author” in evidence basis confers no override.

Mechanism A: opposite polarity attached to one proposition ID, no consuming rule. Mechanism B: opposite polarity on two semantically equivalent proposition records, evading an ID-only grouping. Both require detection independently of scalar multiplicity and installed consuming rules.

## Identity and comparison contract

Work on one resolved candidate snapshot: base-frame universe/continuity/revision plus frozen changes, after structural checks and deterministic event expansion. Exclude superseded/deleted versions from active membership; retain them in history. Never combine another continuity, revision or universe. A record reference resolves by `(universe, record)` in that snapshot; bind its exact active version in the comparison proof. There is no executable general alias/same-person resolver in current source. Names, labels, translations and prose similarity never equate entities or predicates. Distinct entity IDs stay distinct even if their names match. Adding alias inference is outside G05.

An equivalence key comprises resolved subject identity, resolved predicate identity/version, quantification, proposition context, the full named-role binding multiset, and property value (including presence versus absence). Proposition record ID and editorial/source metadata are excluded. Assertions still refer to their original propositions; no record merge or rewrite occurs.

Role vector order is irrelevant; role name and participant identity are material. Preserve multiplicity of repeated bindings under the declared role shape; structural invalidity stays structural. Swapping donor/recipient is not equivalent. Do not infer relation symmetry from names or reorder participants across different roles. Resolve predicate definitions and validate role declarations before normalization. Distinct definitions with the same namespace/key are ambiguous, not equivalent. Universal, existential and particular claims remain distinct.

Exact supported equality uses structural canonical equality or `basic::compare(Eq)` where it gives a definite exact result (for example exact numeric spellings). Do not use approximate, unknown, overlapping range, unit labels, or unsupported conversion as equality. For values with a supported inequality, keys differ. If plausible comparison cannot decide equality, retain an unresolved comparison rather than dropping the pair as unequal. A grouped index may use coarse subject/predicate/context/quantification/role buckets followed by exact value comparison; raw value bytes alone would miss numeric equivalence. Avoid transitive merging unless every member is proven equivalent under the same comparator.

## Context, world, realm and temporal intersection

For exact detection, require each assertion's scope context to match its proposition context. Current structural validation only checks that each independently names a represented context; a mismatch is unresolved/ineligible here, not a new structural ban or permission to rewrite either context. Compare only identical resolved represented contexts. Objective, character belief, dream, source narrative and nested child contexts do not inherit truth from parents. Opposing evidence inside one belief context is still reportable there, but separate witnesses disagreeing in separate contexts are legal and do not create an objective conflict. Policies use an explicit context operand and never default to all contexts.

Applicability is evidence, not ownership or focus. UniverseWide intersects UniverseWide as explicit universal applicability; UniverseWide intersects Worlds as the named set; Worlds intersects Worlds as set intersection, with duplicates removed. Empty world sets have no applicability. Disjoint known sets prove no overlap. Conditional/Unspecified stay unresolved unless an existing supported resolver establishes their applicability; this slice adds no condition interpreter. If another dimension proves disjointness, no uncertain world calculation is needed. A selected UI world only narrows delivery; it cannot establish an assertion's world scope or erase a shared-world conflict from authoritative admission.

Realm identities must match, including both absent for non-realm evidence. Two different explicit realms are isolated. A missing realm is not all realms; mixed absent/present comparison is unresolved when containment would be required. Equal explicit realm tags allow a direct scoped pair report, but do not certify realm-dependent rule inference: current generic claim evaluation cannot do that. Such blocking rule evaluation remains unsupported unless already supported, not silently widened.

Use named-calendar integer ticks and the existing half-open `[start,end)` algebra. Report the actual common interval or instant, not the union of assertion lifetimes. `[0,10)` versus `[10,20)` has no overlap; tick 10 belongs only to the latter. Equal exact instants overlap; a point at an interval's excluded end does not. Empty `[10,10)` contains no instant. Explicit unbounded endpoints remain unbounded; Unknown is never infinity. Different calendars and legacy intervals without a clock stay unresolved; no date conversion is synthesized from labels or descriptive conversion expressions.

Two Unspecified validities preserve the existing no-temporal-claim comparison convention (`time::overlaps` returns true). Label their intersection **unqualified**, without inventing a calendar or claiming overlap at any named tick. Unspecified paired with a dated validity, Unknown, relative times without supported ordering, unsupported persistence/conditions and approximate endpoint units are unresolved. Do not extrapolate persistence or choose the newest assertion as truth.

For bounded endpoint uncertainty, use existing extremal comparison: all interpretations overlap → definite overlap; none overlap → definite separation; mixed → unresolved. A definite existential overlap with uncertain endpoints has an uncertainty-qualified intersection description, not fabricated exact bounds. Exact subcells can be reported only where both polarities definitely apply. Assertion Precision other than Exact or nonempty alternatives cannot establish an exact opposing bit; keep their potentially relevant evidence in unresolved coverage. A definite pair can coexist with other unresolved evidence: preserve its finding and mark the remaining coverage separately.

## Evidence and results

Keep Supported `(1,0)`, Refuted `(0,1)`, Conflicting `(1,1)`, NotEstablished `(0,0)` for the underlying proposition in each applicable cell. Conflict never entails unrelated propositions, consumes sources, resolves truth, or transforms a refutation into another supported value. “No opposing pair found” is a bounded diagnostic about inspected evidence, not a statement that the proposition is true or false.

Recommend a versioned optional `opposing_evidence` collection on Validation, with a closed typed entry containing: diagnostic fingerprint; resolved equivalence description; original proposition IDs/versions; separate support and refutation assertion-version lists; immutable dependency/source chains; represented context; shared applicability/realm; common calendar/cell or unqualified/uncertainty description; completeness and unresolved reason codes; relevant policy rule/version and exception references, if any. Entry kind distinguishes definite overlap from unresolved applicability/equality. Never encode a potential pair as Conflicting. The existing Finding schema requires `rule_id`; do not manufacture a canonical rule ID for a rule-free diagnostic. Actual authored policy findings continue through existing Finding and admission mechanisms.

Canonical ordering is by comparison key, scope/cell and sorted version identities; polarity groups stay separate. Deduplicate repeated witnesses, not independent source chains. Partition changing evidence sets at boundaries, coalescing only adjacent cells with identical evidence and policy/exception disposition. Include definitions/propositions/assertions, event-effect links and source-anchor/derivation dependencies needed to inspect the finding. Source byte verification is distinct from interpretation or truth. If provenance is unsupported, preserve the known direct assertion evidence and expose the chain limitation.

Fingerprint binds candidate digest, base frame, evaluator/comparison version, equivalence, context/world/realm/cell, both polarity chains and applicable policy versions. New assertions, corrections, scope, definitions or evaluator changes produce new bindings. It is not a reusable waiver token.

Authority evaluates the full authorized candidate privately. Apply existing principal/grant visibility before public serialization: every disclosed ID, version, dependency, world, fingerprint and metadata field must be visible. Keep the existing Restricted validation response for scoped clients; do not expose hidden conflicts through a hash, counts, source labels or IDs, and do not emit a “clean” public scan. Full author review can inspect both sides. Fresh commit recheck may use broader private evidence as today, but must not disclose it to the scoped caller. Grant changes invalidate delivery caches and reuse.

## Authored commit policy and correction

The automatic report does not change `Checked.admissible` by itself. Potential pairs make coverage Partial (or Restricted for access limits); existing complete-coverage requirement excludes delegated commit. Exact author approval can admit a nonblocking partially evaluated candidate under current rules, provided no structural/hard-coded/blocking check fails. Show unresolved items during review. An advisory diagnostic is not an approval.

To express D01 without turning every conflict into a structural invariant, recommend one bounded typed expression `NoOpposingEvidence { subject: Operand, predicate: String, context: Operand }`. It compares all complete proposition keys for that bound subject and uniquely resolved predicate in the current world/time, using the same comparison kernel. Existing finite authored quantifiers may bind subjects; no unbounded enumeration or inferred collection completeness. An empty inspected snapshot establishes only absence of an opposing pair in that exact candidate, never absence of facts in the fictional world. This operator's metacondition returns Supported when inspection is complete and no pair exists, Refuted when a definite opposing pair exists, and NotEstablished/incomplete when absence cannot be established. A definite pair plus uncertainty remains Refuted with incomplete coverage. Underlying conflicting propositions retain `(1,1)`; the metacondition does not rewrite their truth values.

Use existing Rule kind, policy, applicability, validity, capabilities and protected author editing. Explicit context operand prevents a belief rule from policing objective reality. `target_predicates` and subject-type hints must not silently broaden or replace expression operands. Register a versioned capability for this operator; unsupported hosts report unsupported and blocking rules fail admission. Existing finite temporal partitioning must include both polarity boundaries and exception endpoints, including unqualified rule validity over dated evidence; evaluating only the submitted frame would miss earlier overlap. Unsupported partitioning stays incomplete.

| Policy | Effect of a violation or unresolved applicable check |
|---|---|
| No consistency rule / RecordFinding / Advisory | Report; no G05 denial. Partial coverage disallows delegation; exact author approval remains available if other checks permit. |
| AlwaysBlock | Failed/incomplete rule blocks author approval and delegated commit. No exception waiver. |
| BlockUnlessException | Blocks unless a valid accepted exception covers every failing cell; retain original finding. Incomplete applicability is not waived into complete evaluation. |
| ScopeEnforced | Preserve current blocking enforcement; no new waiver path. |
| ReportUnsupported | Report unsupported coverage; preserve current admission behavior and complete-coverage delegation gate. |

For BlockUnlessException, require exact rule version, valid context/world/time containment, nonempty rationale and existing author-accepted IssueDecision linkage; apply the existing protected admission path. A standalone Accept/Defer/Resolve IssueDecision, evidence note, source verification flag or direct author approval cannot waive a failed blocking check. The author can instead propose a protected rule revision or a correction, then revalidate and approve that exact candidate. Existing hard-coded scalar, structural, action and completeness denials remain unchanged.

Corrections revise/end/supersede active evidence through reviewed existing operations, never delete historical evidence or prefer a record because it has a later editorial timestamp. Recompute complete candidate groups even for an unrelated proposal, adoption or import; do not scan only newly created assertion IDs. A longstanding conflict remains visible. A correction that ends support at tick 10 and starts refutation at 10 is legal; overlapping old/new evidence at 9 is still found. Prior revision reads retain prior conflict. Changed head, candidate digest, validation or evaluator requires applicable fresh validation/authority; receipts retain their historical meaning.

## Measurable acceptance after approval and implementation

The companion fixture table is a frozen design oracle, independently compared with [review](g05-independent-review.md). It does not claim engine execution. Qualification must achieve:

1. Exact expected detection and scope for every fixture, with zero false conflicts in isolation/non-overlap controls; both failure mechanisms detected without consuming rules. All four truth states and an unrelated absent claim remain correct.
2. Every applicable support/refutation version and dependency present in authorized output; no hidden ID/hash/count/metadata disclosed in restricted output. Original findings remain after exceptions and historical correction reads.
3. Indexed and unindexed results identical for every fixture under reversed insertion order and each cyclic record-order rotation; repeat evidence does not duplicate findings. Distinct-ID numeric equality and named-role order controls both pass.
4. Policy matrix passes through actual propose → validate → private approval/delegated commit routes: unauthorized, inadmissible, stale and incompletely delegated candidates leave head unchanged; valid report-only approval/correction and exact exceptions produce receipts. Test stale rule version, wrong context/world, too-short exception and unsupported operator. No standalone IssueDecision bypass.
5. Revalidation catches preexisting overlap in a candidate containing an unrelated change and overlap introduced by direct assertion, event-generated assertion, revision/adoption and shared import admission. Restart preserves evidence, exact retry receipts and historical membership. Record all failed attempts.
6. Bound work to at most 1,000,000 candidate comparisons and 1,000 diagnostic cells per run initially, aligned with current scalar budgets. Budget exhaustion is explicit incomplete coverage, never clean success or authorization of a blocking rule. Verify a just-below and just-above limit control and indexed/reference agreement. Cancellation and restricted scans remain bounded.
7. Existing scalar, four-valued Boolean, exception, authority and contract regressions pass without weakened expectations; regenerated schemas reject unknown/duplicate fields and unsupported versions. Proposed results and new authored expressions remain readable only on declared compatible hosts.

Approval of this proposal is the remaining human decision. G05 implementation, host/native qualification and gap closure are future work and are not authorized or completed here.
